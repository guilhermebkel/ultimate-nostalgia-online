# Glossary

| Term                          | Meaning                                                                                                                                                  | Avoid                                      |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **uno**                       | The name of the hub and the site. It is also the short form of the expansion. Always lowercase.                                                          | UNO (that is the card game of Mattel), Uno |
| **Ultimate Nostalgia Online** | What "uno" stands for. Written in full in the footer, the title and the description.                                                                     | UNO as an acronym in capitals              |
| **hub**                       | The site at uno.guilherr.me: one page that lists the games.                                                                                              | portal, platform                           |
| **game**                      | A thing the visitor can play or will be able to play, with its own site or repository (Zerod, Hero's Play, Pebo, Ogro Party). One entry of `games.json`. | product, app                               |
| **live**                      | A game that can be played now. It has a url and a "Play now" link.                                                                                       | released, launched                         |
| **soon**                      | A game that is not playable yet. No link, no button, and it stays out of the structured data.                                                            | upcoming, beta                             |
| **featured card**             | The wide card of a live game (today Zerod and Hero's Play).                                                                                              | hero, banner                               |
| **status label**              | The small label of a card ("Out now:", "Coming soon:") with its colored dot.                                                                             | badge, tag                                 |
| **locale**                    | A language of the site. `en` is at `/` and is the default; `pt-br` is at `/pt-br/`. Each is its own page.                                                | translation (as the file)                  |
| **brand kit**                 | The logo, icons and web files in `design/`.                                                                                                              | assets                                     |
| **tile**                      | One of the three letter blocks of the logo (u, n, o).                                                                                                    | block, box                                 |
| **share image**               | `og.png`, 1200×630, shown when a link is shared.                                                                                                         | thumbnail, banner                          |
| **GTM script**                | The owner's Google Tag Manager loader, `https://static.guilherr.me/js/gtm.js`.                                                                           | analytics, tracker                         |
